Delano Loretto Area United Way

For generations, the Delano area has answered the needs of its neighbors with generosity and a strong spirit of community. What began with local business leaders and citizens joining together after World War II eventually grew into the Delano–Loretto Area United Way, part of a nationwide tradition of community-based giving. Through door-to-door campaigns, volunteer leadership, annual fundraising drives, and partnerships with local organizations, the effort has evolved along with the communities it serves. From supporting health and human-service organizations to responding to local families in need and unexpected crises, the story of the Delano–Loretto Area United Way reflects an enduring tradition: neighbors coming together to help neighbors and strengthen their community.
