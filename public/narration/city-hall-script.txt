City of Delano - Presidents & Mayors

For 150 years, Delano's story has been shaped not only by the events that changed the community, but by the people who stepped forward to lead it. From the village presidents of its earliest days to the mayors of the modern city, each generation faced its own challenges and made decisions that helped build the Delano we know today. Streets were laid out, wells dug, lights turned on, water and sewer systems built, parks established, and public buildings raised as a small Minnesota village steadily grew into a thriving city. The stories of Delano's mayors and village presidents offer a glimpse at that journey—and at the local leaders whose service, vision, and everyday decisions helped guide the community through 150 years of change.
