Funeral Services

Funeral customs in Delano have changed greatly since the community’s earliest days, when families prepared and cared for their loved ones at home and local carpenters often supplied the coffins. By the late 1800s and early 1900s, undertaking and embalming had become established professions, with funeral parlors gradually replacing services held in family homes. Minnesota’s first licensed woman embalmer, Mrs. Mattie Rader, was one of Delano’s early undertakers. Including Walter M. Tautges and later the Boyce Funeral Service, they provided generations of families with care during times of loss. The history of funeral services in Delano reflects not only changing customs and professions, but also the enduring role of local people and businesses in caring for their neighbors during some of life’s most difficult moments.
